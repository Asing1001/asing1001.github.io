Self Intro

Thank you for the opportunity to be interviewed for the position today. I’m really excited about this opportunity. Previously, I worked as a Sales Operations Manager at Asurion, an American tech company. My job involved cross-departmental project collaboration. I cooperated with departments like Finance, Legal, Sales, and the Software Development Teams across 3 countries. Before that, I worked at LINE for over four years. LINE is the largest social media app in Asia. At LINE, we offered online advertisement solutions to our clients. As an operations manager there, I was in charge of building standard processes for the ads delivery operations tasks. When it came to new product launches, I also took responsibility for the UAT program to ensure the products’ systems would work smoothly. Overall, my background highlights my experience in tech companies with large user bases. I also have extensive experience working in global companies. I have studied the job description, and my skills, qualities, and strengths are an excellent match for the position.

Campaign Management System - Build SOP

Situation

“When I worked at LINE, our sales operation team needed to manage a variety of ad solutions, each with different limitations and rules. For example, some products could only be booked twice a week, while others could only pop up between 10 AM and 6 PM. Managing these diverse ad policies manually was time-consuming and error-prone.”

Task

“As an operator, I realized that we needed a more efficient system to manage these ad slots and reduce the manual workload.”

Action

“I initially came up with the idea of using a system to manage all types of ad resources. We were already using different Google Sheets to manage various ad resources, so I collected these sheets from different operators. I analyzed and sourced the logic behind the different ads. Then, I approached the development team to discuss how we could develop this system.”

Points

Situation

When I worked at LINE, I took charge of a project to localize our global product delivery operations across multiple departments and countries.

Task

My primary task was to enhance the market competitiveness of our product by localizing our system operations. This involved migrating the operational processes from a global framework to a more streamlined local approach. One of the main challenges was convincing the Korean team, led by the product owner, who had concerns about potential human errors with increased user volume on the localized system.

Action

To address the various concerns from our international colleagues, I:

  • Developed a comprehensive migration plan outlining each step of the process.
  • Held weekly meetings to ensure clear and consistent communication among all stakeholders.
  • Conducted simulations to identify and mitigate potential issues beforehand.
  • Performed thorough testing to ensure a seamless transition and minimize any operational disruptions.

Result

This approach not only mitigated concerns but also led to a seamless transition, enhancing operational efficiency by 50% and bolstering our market position significantly.

CRM-Persona Project

Situation

“When I worked at LINE, our platform offered multiple services and collected extensive user data, including user behavior, preferences, and hobbies. We aimed to leverage this data to allow advertisers to target specific audiences more effectively.”

Task

“As an operations manager, I was responsible for expanding our CRM functionality and enhancing our existing system. This involved collaborating with stakeholders to prioritize which data to collect and which tagging strategies to implement first.”

Action

“To prioritize our efforts, we focused on identifying categories of data that would maximize revenue potential, aligning with our North Star metric. We decided to start with e-commerce customer data due to our platform hosting e-commerce shops, providing us with rich user behavior insights that were more accurate compared to other platforms.”

Result

“As a result of our strategic approach, we successfully implemented targeted tagging for e-commerce customer data. This allowed our clients to personalize campaigns more effectively, leading to a significant increase in ROI by 150%. Additionally, our expanded CRM capabilities enabled us to better utilize customer data across our platform.”

CRM Project

Situation

“In 2021, major ad platforms like Google and Apple announced changes to their third-party cookies policies. Our goal was to assist advertisers on our platform in collecting their own data for future ad targeting.”

Task

“I gathered business requirement documents (BRDs) from our sales teams: direct sales, reseller teams, and SMB teams, each with unique priorities.”

Action

“I developed a project prioritization framework based on strategic alignment, technical feasibility, and resource requirements. This framework guided me in prioritizing the development of SMB functionalities.”

Result

“Implementing a feature allowing merchants to distribute their own membership cards significantly enhanced our CRM capabilities. This initiative led to a 67% increase in SMB official accounts, rising from 30,000 to 50,000 accounts. Moreover, it generated over $100,000 in additional revenue in the second half of the year.”

LSP

Situation

I launched the company’s first Online to Offline (O2O) business solution. This project required coordinating demands between the sales team, internal development team, and outsourced third-party vendors. As an Operations Manager, I was responsible for designing a detailed plan for all stakeholders.

Action

To succeed in such a complex project, I created clear user stories to ensure everyone consistently understood the product’s appearance and functionality. I divided the project development into phases, focusing on core elements in each phase. I also set up weekly check-in meetings to ensure every department was aligned and working cohesively. Additionally, I planned and executed User Acceptance Testing (UAT) to ensure the product met expectations.

Result

We successfully launched the product within six months, generating over $500,000 in revenue in the first year.

The product was highly successful upon its launch.

Action

I proactively suggested to my supervisor that the product’s system flexibility should have the opportunity for expansion. This idea came from the other project I worked on. I believed that combining the reward delivery system with another product could increase the market potential.

Result

As it involved integrating two existing system functionalities, we completed the system upgrade in just three months. The upgraded product not only addressed the pain points of the food and beverage industry but also aligned with the business models of customers in beauty, real estate, and e-commerce sectors. This resulted in the product contributing to revenue exceeding 3 times than expectations.

Challenge in Project

Situation

While developing LINE’s first O2O business solution, we found that integrating with Offline retailer’s channel systems would take much longer than expected. We initially planned to integrate with three major retail channels, but our team realized it would take over a year, whereas we needed to launch in six months.

Task

I needed to figure out how to still meet our deadline. I thought of two options: reduce the integration to one channel or find another faster way. I remembered the e-commerce department had done a similar project, so I reached out to a colleague there.

Action

My colleague suggested a third-party vendor already integrated with all the retailers’ POS systems. I set up meetings, and we decided to partner with this vendor. Their existing integrations meant we could speed up our process significantly.

Result

We finished the integration a week ahead of schedule and launched in over 10 retail channels, more than expected. This gave our product a stronger market presence and more opportunities for success.

Data Analysis Help Growth (Asurion)

Situation

When I worked at Asurion as a Sales Operations Manager, I was tasked with leveraging data to uncover insights and define the market strategy in collaboration with the COO and CFO.

Task

Our business model involved providing a subscription-based product service to our users. To seize market opportunities, I needed to analyze key performance indicators, including User Resources, Growth Rate, Daily Active Users (DAU), Retention Rate, Churn Rate, and Lifetime Value (LTV), to monitor and assess our product performance.

Action

I conducted a detailed analysis of our user base using tools like Excel for data aggregation and Tableau for data visualization. This analysis revealed several key insights:

  • User Resources and Growth Rate: Identified that a significant portion of our growth came from the app store where telecom partnerships.
  • Churn Rate and LTV: Showed higher churn rates for users without telecom partnerships.

Based on these insights, I proposed a strategic partnership with the largest telecom company in the region. This proposal was backed by a detailed report demonstrating how the partnership could increase our DAU, improve retention rates, and lower churn rates by providing users with more integrated and convenient services.

Result

After 10 months of preparation, including negotiations and aligning the project scope with both companies’ objectives, we successfully signed a five-year contract with the telecom company. This partnership led to a 70% increase in user subscriptions, significantly enhancing our user base and generating substantial revenue growth.

Why Operations Manager (Ally)

I enjoy being an operations manager because it is the role I am most comfortable in. Leading optimization programs always empowers me, and I enjoy the problem-finding and solving procedures.

Situation

When I worked at Ally Transport, I needed to establish standard operating procedures for a start-up company’s operations.

Challenge

There was a communication gap between the software development team and the warehouse logistics team.

Action

I spent a month working hands-on at the warehouse site, collaborating with dispatch personnel and operations managers to understand logistics operations in detail. I presented data using flowcharts and swimlane diagrams to visualize processes, providing a clear foundation for the internal team. I then collaborated with the development team to ensure dispatch logic alignment and established a new dispatching system.

Result

As a result, this project completed in 6 months and improved operational efficiency by 50%. It makes me feel that I am contributing to the company directly and gives me a sense of achievement.

Disagreements/ Conflict

In my previous role, when gathering business requirement documents from our sales teams—direct sales, resellers,

and SMB teams—we encountered differing perspectives on how to optimize our system. Each team had valid points and unique priorities based on their market insights and customer interactions.

To manage this, I adopted a collaborative approach. First, I actively listened to each team’s proposals and concerns, ensuring I understood their goals thoroughly. I facilitated transparent communication by clearly outlining the criteria we used for prioritization, such as strategic alignment with company goals, technical feasibility, and potential impact on revenue.

During prioritization discussions, I encouraged open dialogue where teams could share their insights and discuss trade-offs. I leveraged data and metrics to objectively assess each proposal’s potential impact, which helped us make informed decisions that aligned with the company’s goal.

By building a collaborative environment and seeking consensus, we were able to prioritize development efforts effectively. This approach not only resolved conflicts but also strengthened teamwork and alignment across sales teams.

Result

As a result of this approach, we successfully implemented initiatives that significantly enhanced our CRM capabilities and drove measurable increases in SMB engagement and revenue. It underscored the importance of balancing diverse perspectives while maintaining focus on achieving our strategic goals.

Disagree with Supervisor

Situation

When I joined Asurion, I was tasked with overseeing the mobile insurance product. Upon reviewing the budget, my manager suggested leveraging vendor-managed social media campaigns for branding.

Action

Considering that our product follows a B2B2C business model, where social media content may not directly influence consumer purchases, I collected data to show that 80% of the revenue is driven from resellers. So I proposed to redirect the marketing budget towards incentive programs for distributors to directly stimulate sales incentives, which would better align with this year’s goals.

Result

We exceeded performance targets by 30%, and the management expressed satisfaction with the outcome of the budget reallocation.

My Strength

Adaptability and Leadership

I embrace change and challenges positively. For instance, at LINE, we were about to launch a completely new advertorial product. I jumped at the chance to take charge. This experience not only involved operating a product but also building a project from scratch. I believe this is a highly valuable skill, especially when working in a fast-paced company.

Quick Learning and Cross-functional Communication

I’m a quick learner. When I started working in a tech company, I wanted to improve communication with the development team, so I taught myself programming. This self-initiated learning helped me understand their work better and build strong relationships across teams. This ability to quickly learn and adapt has been crucial in fostering effective cross-functional collaboration.

Weakness

My natural inclination towards building strong relationships sometimes led me to take on too many responsibilities from colleagues, including salespersons and project managers. Initially, I attempted to solve every issue myself, which eventually led to burnout and affected my personal performance. Recognizing this, I decided to establish a prioritization framework. I now prioritize tasks aligned with my core responsibilities first, ensuring I deliver effectively in my role. For tasks that are more peripheral or involve helping others, I redirect colleagues to appropriate resources where they can find solutions independently. This adjustment has enabled me to maintain focus on my core responsibilities while continuing to foster positive relationships and uphold my performance reputation.

入職後最大挑戰

By reading and studying the job description, I could grasp the type of work I will be doing in the early days in this role. The first challenge will be quickly yet diligently catching up on and implementing the operational policies and procedures. In a fast-paced software company, there’s no time to waste. However, in a large international company like TikTok, operations often involve multiple departments and even multiple countries. If I am hired, I plan to do some homework to help myself quickly get up to speed with the team and the environment. I will also give myself a 3-month plan to quickly get into the environment.

5-Year Plan

In five years, I plan to still be working for the company. I would either be in this role with extra responsibilities or have earned a promotion to a higher level. In five years, I aim to be an expert in my area and to help train new team members, demonstrating my leadership abilities.

Why TikTok

I’m very excited about this position, mainly for the following two reasons:

  1. The first reason is that TikTok is an app that is clearly popular among end users, which means the company has clear rules and guidelines in respect to how operations should not only be run but also well-managed.
